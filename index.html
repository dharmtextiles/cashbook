For per meter or per day work you can add several rate lines in one period (for example 3000 m × ₹2.2 and 2000 m × ₹2.4). Total is the sum of all lines. For Monthly salary people the per-day rate is the monthly salary ÷ 30 (always 30, whatever the month length), and you enter the days worked. On Other expense each month starts empty: names are added for that month only and are not carried to the next month. “+ Add person” adds someone to this period only (pick an existing person, or create a new one), and × at the end of a row removes that row from this period only. Payable = Total − Total Advance + Other, rounded to the nearest multiple you type in “Round payable to” (blank means no rounding). Days for per-day and monthly people come from the Attendance page automatically (type the days yourself for anyone not added to attendance; a monthly salary counts at most 30 days a month, or 15 in a half-month); type your own days to override, or tap “Use attendance” to go back. The page opens on the current period: Monthly switches on the 11th of each month; Half-month switches to the 1st half on the 11th and to the 2nd half on the 26th. Every payment you add in the Cash book for a person in the Work list appears here as Advance. After you tick Paid, that period is locked and new payments go to the next period.

Attendance Everyone is Present by default

Tap a day once for Absent (A), again for Half day (½), again to go back to Present (P). “Holiday for all” marks everyone Absent on the date you choose (tap a day number to pick it); then tap a person’s day to change them. Days for per-day and monthly people go to Pagar patrak automatically. Per-meter people are not counted there. “Leave” hides a person from this page; the name stays in Pagar patrak until the salary is completed.
